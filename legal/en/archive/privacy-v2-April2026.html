Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled across the mobile applications and games published by Mohan Sah (operating as Mohan Sah Studios) on the Google Play Store (collectively referred to as "our Games"). This includes titles such as HandCricket, Jumper Jam, and others.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a better gameplay experience, we may collect the following information:

User-Provided & Technical Data

  • Usernames: Collected for displaying High Scores and local leaderboards.
  • Gameplay Data: Progress, scores, and in-game currency/tokens.
  • Device Identifiers: We may access non-sensitive device IDs (like the Android ID) for analytics and crash reporting.
Note: We do not maintain a centralized, external database for permanent storage of personal identity. Most gameplay data is processed locally on your device.

Third-Party Service Providers (Google AdMob)

Our Games use Google AdMob for advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data including IP addresses, Advertising IDs, and device information.

For more details, review Google's Privacy & Terms.

2. Children’s Privacy & Families Policy (COPPA/GDPR Compliance)

We take children’s privacy very seriously and comply with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

  • Ad Filtering: In Games directed at children, we only use Google Play Certified Ad SDKs. We ensure that ads served to children are age-appropriate and do not involve interest-based tracking.
  • Neutral Age Screen: Certain Games may implement a "Neutral Age Screen" to identify the user's age and apply higher privacy protections to younger players.
  • No Personal Data: We do not knowingly collect, use, or disclose personally identifiable information (such as real names, addresses, or phone numbers) from children under the age of 13.
  • Parental Rights: Parents can request the review or deletion of any data associated with their child by contacting us at the email address provided below.

3. Advertising & Monetization

Our Games may include:

  • Banner, Interstitial, & App Open Ads: Ads shown at transitions or startup.
  • Rewarded Ads: Optional ads that grant in-game rewards.

We may offer a "Remove Ads" feature via In-App Purchase. These transactions are handled securely by the Google Play Store.

4. Security

We use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, though no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.
