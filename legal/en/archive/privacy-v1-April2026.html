Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled across the mobile applications and games published by Mohan Sah on the Google Play Store (collectively referred to as "our Games"). This includes, but is not limited to, titles such as HandCricket, WordleQuest, and cengiz-pz-admob-test.

Our Games are built using the Godot Engine and utilize third-party plugins (such as AdMob) to provide features and advertisements.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a better gameplay experience, we may collect the following information across our Games:

User-Provided & Technical Data

  • Usernames: We may collect usernames (randomly generated or provided by you) primarily for displaying High Scores and local leaderboards.
  • Gameplay Data & High Scores: We track progress, scores, and in-game currency/tokens to facilitate game mechanics.
  • Device Identifiers: We may access unique device IDs (such as the Android ID or Advertising ID) for internal analytics, crash reporting, and to ensure proper functioning.
Note: Currently, this gameplay data is primarily processed locally on your device. We do not maintain a centralized, external private database for permanent storage of personal identity at this time.

Third-Party Service Providers (Google AdMob)

Our free Games use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data including:

  • IP addresses
  • Advertising IDs (e.g., Google Advertising ID)
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • App usage and interaction data

For more details on how Google processes this data, please review Google's Privacy & Terms.

2. Advertising & Monetization

To keep our Games accessible, we display various types of advertisements. Depending on the Game, formats may include:

  • Banner & Native Advanced Ads: Inline ads that match the game's layout.
  • Interstitial & App Open Ads: Full-page ads at natural transitions or during startup.
  • Rewarded Ads: Optional ads that grant you in-game tokens/currency or hints for viewing.

Ad-Free Option (In-App Purchases)

Certain Games may offer a "Remove Ads" feature. If you choose to pay a one-time fee to stop advertisements, the transaction is handled entirely and securely by the Google Play Store. We do not see, process, or store your credit card or payment information.

3. Children's Privacy

Our Games are intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take immediate steps to delete this data.

4. Security

We value your trust. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting your information, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect new games or features. You are advised to review this page periodically. Changes are effective immediately when posted on this page.
