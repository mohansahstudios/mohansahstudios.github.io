Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled across the mobile applications and games published by Mohan Sah (operating as Mohan Sah Studios) on the Google Play Store. This includes titles such as HandCricket, Jumper Jam, and others.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a better gameplay experience and support in-app features, we may collect the following data:

User-Provided & Technical Data

  • Usernames & High Scores: Tracked to facilitate local or competitive leaderboards.
  • Purchase History: If you use In-App Purchases (e.g., "Remove Ads"), we track the transaction status via the Google Play Billing API to provide the purchased service. We do not collect or store financial or credit card information.
  • App Performance & Activity: We may collect crash logs, diagnostics, and app interaction data (such as levels reached) to improve game performance and debug technical issues.

Third-Party Service Providers (Google AdMob & Firebase)

Our Games use Google AdMob and may use Firebase for analytics. These services may collect:

  • Device Identifiers: Advertising IDs used for ad attribution and analytics.
  • Approximate Location: Derived from IP addresses to provide localized and relevant advertisements.

2. Children’s Privacy & Families Policy

We comply with COPPA and GDPR. For games directed at children:

  • Neutral Age Screen: We may use a local age-gate. This data is used only to determine app behavior (such as showing non-personalized ads) and is not sent to our servers.
  • Ad Safety: We only show ads from Google Play Certified Ad Networks for apps in the Families program.

3. Data Retention & Deletion Requests

You can delete local gameplay data by uninstalling the app. For other requests:

  • Purchase Data: Managed by your Google Play Account settings.
  • Deletion Requests: Email mohansahstudios+support@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request".
